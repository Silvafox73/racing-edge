How value is measured

Implied probability

For decimal odds: 1 ÷ decimal odds. Fractional odds are converted before comparison.

Estimated edge

Your estimated win probability minus the market-implied probability. Estimates need reliable form and odds data before they can be trusted.

Value calculator

Enter a horse's odds and your own estimated chance. Use a realistic estimate, not the horse's odds alone.

This calculator is educational. Odds can move, bookmakers include a margin, and your probability estimate may be wrong. It does not place bets.

About Racing Edge

Built in stages

Version 1 gives us the Android-friendly app shell, a sample shortlist, a value calculator and a simple results tracker.

Next: connect a legitimate free racing-data source, then build and test the form-rating model. Free sources may have usage limits and may not provide bookmaker prices.

Responsible use

Horse racing involves risk. Never stake money you cannot afford to lose. No prediction system can guarantee winners or profit. Set limits and avoid chasing losses.
